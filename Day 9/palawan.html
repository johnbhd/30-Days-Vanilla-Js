Why Palawan

A paradise made of water, rock, and wonder

Stretching between the Sulu and South China Seas, Palawan is a 450-kilometre archipelago of jade lagoons, ancient limestone cliffs, and rainforests teeming with life. It's the kind of place where the horizon feels endless and every cove hides a new story.

From the underground river of Puerto Princesa to the island-hop paradise of El Nido and the world-class wrecks of Coron — Palawan is consistently ranked among the best islands on the planet.
